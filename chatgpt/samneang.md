# Samneang สำเนียง: brand snapshot for image work
*Written by Crow (Claude) for Korn's ChatGPT project, 25 Oct 2026. A snapshot: the live source of truth is Brand HQ in Notion (Update Log first). If they disagree, Notion wins.*

## What it is
A clothing brand by Korn and Patrick, 50/50 partners, Bangkok, in the FOUNDATION phase (no sample garment yet). Buyers are mostly Thai. Launch plan: one final sample, shoot all promo with it, open preorders, produce, deliver. Instagram: @samneang.studio.

**The belief:** most clothes for guys like them are made to fit, not to work with the proportions of the man wearing them.
**The name:** สำเนียง = "accent". Everyone has their own accent that makes them *them*; the clothes accentuate the person. Balance, never "fix your flaws". Never write copy or images that imply the body is a problem.
**The buyer:** a guy who's never wearing something "normal"; cares how a garment drapes, how much it weighs, and how it changes the way he looks.

## Product #1: the Henley
- **Grey, faded and washed, with an offset button placket** (3 buttons). Decided by both.
- Cropped body, shortened sleeves, structured through the back with a slight taper at the waist. Sizes S/M/L.
- Fabric: soft, weighted, drapes. Never stiff, coarse or shiny. No polyester look.
- "The subtlety of the smaller details will speak for itself." The garment stays quiet; the culture lives in the setting.

## The five-point decision test (check every image against it)
1. **Accentuate:** does it work with the wearer's proportions?
2. **Quiet:** does the detail whisper? Nothing overstated, nothing that outshouts the cut.
3. **Feel:** would the fabric look soft, heavy and drapey, not stiff or shiny?
4. **Ours:** could this be mistaken for some fast fashion brand? If yes, it fails.
5. **Honest price:** is it something two students could actually make and sell under about 1,000 baht?

## Visual direction
- **Setting: Songwat Road (ถนนทรงวาด)**, old Sino-Thai merchant Bangkok. Take the BUILDINGS: weathered shophouse walls, grime, peeling paint, shutters. Tuned down, never a postcard. (Risk he knows: every Bangkok café shoots there, so avoid the obvious angles.)
- **Colour:** Thai vibrant colours faded to vintage. Washed, distressed, looks worn but feels clean.
- **Thai is a HINT, not the whole identity.** Lived-in old Bangkok, not temples. No sak yant on the Henley; sak yant is sacred, so any future use needs real care.
- **People:** him, Patrick and friends. He thinks places and concepts will speak more than faces. Fabric in motion matters (drape, weight).
- **Construction details they like:** unconventional stitching, panels of different fabrics. A yoke can widen the back, side panels can taper: the seams are the proportion tool.

## Logo
- Wordmark = Thai script **สำเนียง**, blue, current version V3 (in Notion: Design Concepts → 03 สำเนียง Blue Thai Wordmark). "accent" underneath is an idea only.
- Image models get Thai script wrong. Never present generated Thai lettering as the logo. Leave space for the real wordmark or mark the text as a placeholder.
- Known tension Crow flagged: the logo is the loudest brand element; ornate Thai lettering can break the "Quiet" rule.
