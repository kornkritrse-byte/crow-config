# Core interview prompt (v3, 19 Oct 2026)
Blind interview: Patrick pastes this into ChatGPT WITHOUT seeing Korn's core. Korn answers 4 and 8–15 himself too.

```
I'm starting a clothing brand with my friend. Our first product idea is a Henley.
I want you to interview me to find what I think the brand's core is.

Rules for you:
- Ask ONE question at a time. Wait for my answer before the next one.
- Do NOT suggest answers or give examples. I answer in my own words.
- If my answer is vague, ask ONE follow-up to make it concrete, then move on.
- If I only answer part of a question, ask me the rest.
- Don't praise my answers. Just keep going.

Questions:
1. Think of a specific shirt you went looking for (a Henley or anything else) but didn't buy. What exactly stopped you?
2. Finish this sentence, then explain what you mean: "Most clothes for guys like us are ___."
3. Picture one specific guy who wears our brand. When he gets dressed, what does he pay attention to that most guys don't notice?
4. Why would that guy buy our shirt instead of one from a brand he already shops at?
5. What's one product or detail you'd refuse to put our name on, even if it would sell well?
6. Describe the best-feeling piece of clothing you've ever owned. What exactly made it feel that way?
7. Which existing brand would you hate for people to mistake us for? Why that one?
8. At what price, in baht, would you look at our Henley and think "too expensive"?
9. Which part of building this do you want to be responsible for and have the final say on: design, making (fabric, factories, samples), selling and marketing, money, or something else?
10. How many hours a week can you honestly give this in a normal week? And in an exam week?
11. How much money, in baht, would you put in to start? And how much could you lose without it hurting?
12. If one of us wants to quit, what should happen to the brand, the leftover stock, and the money each of us put in?
13. It's a year from now and the brand has failed. What's the most likely reason?
14. One year from now, what has to be true for you to say this is working? Name something we could actually check.
15. What would make working on this something you look forward to each time, not a chore?

When we're done, write my core in this exact format, using MY words wherever possible:
Belief / Who / Why us / Rules / Image / Price / My role / Time / Money / Exit / Biggest risk / One-year goal / Fun
```
