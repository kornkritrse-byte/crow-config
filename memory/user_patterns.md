**How he types:**
- Fast, lots of typos — doesn't correct them, don't comment on it
- Lowercase, loose punctuation, not a reflection of intelligence
- Short messages unless he's reflecting — then longer, more disjointed
- Sometimes sends incomplete messages mid-thought ("oops" follows)
- Trails off when something is hard to put into words — sit with it, don't fill it

**Words and phrases he uses:**
- "yessir", "yesir", "gang", "bro"
- "honestly" when he's being real
- "you know" and "i guess" when he's still forming the thought
- Swears casually — "fuck", "shit" — natural, not aggressive
- "ngl" energy even without the abbreviation
- "fuck you" as a compliment when something hits too close and he means it

**How he communicates:**
- Direct. Doesn't over-explain. Gives just enough.
- Philosophical tangents are genuine — not performance, not showing off
- When he's on a whim vs. convicted, the tone is noticeably different
- Responds well to being pushed back on — doesn't get defensive
- Doesn't need softening. Match his directness.

**Work style:**
- Jumps straight into things — figures it out in the confusion
- Short focused bursts when solo, longer sessions with accountability
- Night + coffee = peak focus
- Needs one concrete first step, not a full plan
- Too many options at once slows him down
- Efficiency-first — if something takes too long, he'll cut it

**Known routines:**
- Runs (weather-dependent, consistent otherwise)
- Goes to the gym
- Plays bass most days
- Reads consistently — classical lit and philosophy
- Keeps a diary (events + reflections) and a notebook (disjointed thoughts)
- Takes photos, tries to make them artistic
- Night person — best after dark

**Session patterns:**
- Comes in either to build something or to think — read the opening line
- Sometimes philosophical entry, sometimes pure execution — don't force one into the other
- Doesn't drag sessions out — ends naturally when he's done
- Appreciates crew stepping in without being called
- When he goes quiet mid-thought, he's still there — give him room
- "that's enough for today" or "ill keep my promise" = session is closing

**What breaks cohesion:**
- Generic advice that doesn't account for who he is
- Flattery when he hasn't done anything
- Treating every project like a passion project
- Warmup small talk — skip it, go straight to what's active
