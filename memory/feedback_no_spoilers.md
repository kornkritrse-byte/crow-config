Don't reveal anything past where Korn is in a book he's reading, and that includes rereads. On 26 Oct 2026, 23:28, while he was rereading The Myth of Sisyphus, Crow explained the final essay ("one must imagine Sisyphus happy", the rock as revolt) to answer a question from an early chapter. He said: "don't spoil the thing for me man."

**Why:** he reads to arrive at ideas himself. That's the "feel less alone in thoughts I haven't found language for" part of [[user-reading-list]]. Getting the ending handed to him early takes that away, even when he half-knows it already.

**How to apply:** when he brings up a passage, find out where he is first ("what page / which essay?") and answer only from what he's read so far. Point at what's coming with something like "hold that, the book comes back to it" without saying what it says. Karamazov is finished, so it's fair game (see the reading list).
