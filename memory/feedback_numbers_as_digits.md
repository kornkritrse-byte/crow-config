In drills with numbers (AC311, FN201, any calculation), write every number as digits with commas, e.g. 10,000,000 · 300,000 · 5,333,333. Never spell them out ("ten million", "three point three three"). This **overrides** the Room's voice-reply habit of saying numbers the way you'd speak them: in a voice reply, still use digits in the text, and also put the question's figures in a fenced block so they stay on screen.

**Why:** Korn, 29 Oct 2026 13:20, mid AC311 voice drill: spelled-out numbers are hard to read and compare. He asked for "exact numbers so it's easier for me to understand them and compare them."

**How to apply:** every calculation question, answer and correction. The fenced block holds the givens (cost, life, residual, units), one per line. Related: [[feedback-drill-format]].
