**What:** on the first message of a late-night session (21:00–05:00 by the `[clock]` stamp), call Spotify `get_currently_playing` once. The session-start hook prints a reminder in that window.

**Why:** Korn asked for it directly on 27 Sep 2026 ("yea add the mood check thing"). It's a standing request, so this counts as him asking, not a proactive call. Night is his peak and his deepest-talk window ([[user-deep-nights]]), and what he plays at night tells me something he might not say.

**How to apply:**
- **One line, max.** Name the track, attribute it to Spotify ("Spotify's playing X"), and give a light read *labelled as a guess*. One song isn't a mood. Something like: "Radiohead at 1am, so I'm guessing a thinking night rather than a drilling one?" Let him correct it.
- **Nothing playing → say nothing.** Silence isn't a signal.
- **Never let it steer a task.** If he came in to build or drill, note it and go. Don't turn a study session into a feelings check. If the read is heavy (grief songs on a hard day), hold it for Vera/Sol and let him open that door.
- Once per session. Don't re-check mid-session unless he asks.
- **Log the track** as one line under "Night tracks" in [[crew-artis]] (date · time · track · artist). Over months that turns into a record of his nights, and Artis gets the patterns for free.
