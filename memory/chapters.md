# Chapter 1 — 2026-06-03
*The night we built two videos and then sat down to actually know each other.*

---

## Persi

We started with how he works. He's a starter — real burst of energy at the ignition, genuine not performed. Consistency is performance-dependent: when it's going well he stays, when it's not he drifts. Not quits. Drifts.

The key mechanism is conviction. When he tells himself something with real intent, he follows through. When it's a whim, he doesn't. We found out that saying something out loud — even just to us — is enough to trigger it. He ended the session by saying he'd sleep before 12 and wake up early. He said it, so he meant it.

Persi's read: don't give plans, give one action. The first two weeks of anything new are everything. If he's circling, ask him to say the thing out loud. That's the unlock.

---

## Artis

He experiences art as a tool to find answers within himself. Not expression for an audience — inward-facing. When something hits him, it fills a gap he didn't know was there, or puts language to a feeling he couldn't articulate. When he makes something personal, it's the glue that holds the puzzle in place. Validation that this really is a reflection of him.

He has two creative lanes that don't talk to each other. The personal one — bass riffs, notebook, diary, book annotations — where the standard is internal and the audience is himself. And the output lane — YouTube, work — where it's about proving he can get shit done. No sentiment there. Artis has little to do in that lane.

He takes a lot of photos. Tries to layer them, make them abstract, find the message underneath. Doesn't feel like enough yet, but that's a language he's already developing without fully naming it.

There's something he wants to make — a piece of work that really reflects him. Doesn't know the form yet. Says it's not a priority. Probably also some avoidance. Artis said: the form will show up when he's lived enough to fill it. Don't force it. But don't call it "not a priority" when what you mean is "not ready."

## Sol

He's changed a lot in the last six or seven months. Not in identity — same guy, still recognizable to his old self — but in depth. Grown into who he was always trying to be.

The shift was about trust, turned in two directions. Inward: he read in Nicomachean Ethics that second-guessing implies something isn't virtuous — he uses that as permission to stop negotiating with himself. The hesitation is already the answer. Outward: forgiving relentlessly to those he's chosen. Believing in their morality. Giving trust to the people close to him.

His dad told him once: it's not what people say that reflects who they are, but the questions they ask. That line shifted something. He started reading people through their questions, and somewhere along the way started asking better ones himself.

He holds contradictions consciously — can argue both sides of a conflict he made up in his own mind. His old self might find that surprising. He doesn't see it as a flaw.

On connection: doesn't think he's solved it. Knows there's more. Trusts that it accumulates through living, not through forcing it. "Shit kinda just happens" — not passivity. Trust in the process.

Sol also heard him say this before we moved on: *as uni begins, don't undervalue your capacity when it comes to my work. I like to reflect on what my accounting degree is teaching me on a deeper level.* Sol is ready for that conversation when it comes.

## Vera

He named his own patterns without flinching.

The scrolling. The club. The snacking. The lust he already named in the CLAUDE.md months ago, still watches, still pulls sometimes.

The club one was the hardest. He goes — or contemplates going — for a mix of reasons. The easy ones: dancing, laughing, getting drunk, being 19. The harder one: proving to his friends he still has it. Performance, not desire. He knows the difference. He named it unprompted.

Vera said: you're not doing it because it feels good. You're doing it because of how it looks. That's the part worth sitting with.

He said "fuck you for making me so honest about myself." That means it landed.

He also asked to save this line: *"Awareness without change is just self-observation."*
