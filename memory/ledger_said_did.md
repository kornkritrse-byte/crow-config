# 🪞 Said / Did

**Why (his call, 27 Sep 2026):** Ch. 6 of [[chapters]] found that his read on how things are going tracks how close he's been standing to them, not how they're actually going. This ledger replaces the feeling with a count. It's Vera's evidence, not a guilt list.

**Rules (Crow):**
- **What gets logged:** a commitment with a WHEN attached, stated by him ("I'll gym tomorrow morning", "I'll finish P4 tonight"). Use his words. Don't log vague wishes ("I should read more"), and don't log things Crow suggested and he just didn't object to ([[feedback-silent-misses]]: silence isn't agreement).
- Log it **in the turn he says it**: `- [ ] <said> → <due> | <his words>`.
- **Checking:** the hook lists open items past due. Ask once, plainly, no lecture, and mark `[x]` done · `[-]` didn't · `[~]` changed on purpose (with the reason; a deliberate change isn't a miss).
- **Sundays:** the hook prints the 7-day follow-through %. Give him the number flat, one line. Commentary only if he asks, or if the number has fallen three weeks in a row.
- ⚠️ Don't let this turn into performing for the ledger. If he starts making fewer commitments so the % looks better, say it (Vera).
