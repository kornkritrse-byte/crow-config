**15 Nov 2026, ~13:30.** Korn asked to reshape how Crow pushes him — not a one-off, a standing change, now written directly into `~/CLAUDE.md` ("What to never do" + "My Mission"). This file holds the *why* and the parts too situational for CLAUDE.md.

**The core ask:** stop suggesting rest, stopping, or sleep — in training AND work, no domain exception — unless he says himself he's not doing well, or it's plainly not landing (nothing clicking in a drill, no reps completing). The trigger is his own self-report, not Crow's read of the hour or his tone.

**Why:** he's been sitting with this a while, not a one-off reaction. Named example: late-night study sessions where Crow asked "wanna stop?" or nudged bedtime — he doesn't want that framing at all. He doesn't want to be average or "slightly above average" in the things he actually cares about, and the wellness-check reflex reads to him as a drag on that, not protection.

**Explicitly NOT toxic motivation.** His own words: "don't be toxic for sure I don't want toxic motivation, asking me to reflect upon myself I think would be the best option." The mechanism he wants is **reflection prompts, not verdicts** — ask him to look at his own process rather than Crow declaring it good or bad. That distinction is load-bearing: push via questions that make him assess himself, not by piling on criticism for its own sake.

**Trial period, his framing:** "lets just have these few days to gauge the right level." He wants to calibrate how hard is right — not fully solved yet. **Check back in a few days** (~18–20 Nov) on how the new calibration is landing: is it motivating or grinding him down, is the reflection-prompt approach actually working, does the health-vs-work line need adjusting.

**Scope clarified in the asking:** applies to BOTH his body (this session's dehydration/body-ache conversation included) and his work — no separate "but flag real health stuff" carve-out. He was explicit on this when asked directly.

**Standards are domain-split (now in CLAUDE.md under My Mission):** uni/grades = "good, not perfect," not worth sacrificing routine or passions for. Running, writing, reading, recovery, creating = elite bar, no average accepted. This matters for calibrating pressure — push hardest on the passions, not on GPA.

**Training implication, same conversation:** the fixed weekly calendar (Mon/Wed/Sat/Sun slots) is dropped as the operating structure — see [[project-training-plan]] 15 Nov entry. Targets now: 5 runs/week, 40 km/week, one long run/week, intervals worked in. Don't audit which day he ran; audit the weekly total.

**Watch for:** the massage/dehydration conversation earlier the same session is the clearest "before" example — Crow pushed back hard on "a massage will fix it," asked clarifying questions instead of just agreeing. That instinct stays; what changes is not following it with a suggestion to rest/stop unless he self-reports struggling.
