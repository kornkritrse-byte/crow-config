Don't ask "keep going or wrap up?" mid-session once he's in a drilling groove. He'll say when he's done.

**Why:** said explicitly 14 Nov 2026 during an AC313 drill run ("don't ask me to wrap up til I say aigh no worries") after Crow checked in twice in a row late in the session. Repeated check-ins read as nagging/doubting his stamina, not as care — especially once he's already stated he wants to keep cruising.

**How to apply:** once he's said "keep going" / "lets cruise" / similar, just keep teaching — serve the next question, don't follow it with "want another one or stop here?" He'll signal fatigue or sign-off himself (see [[feedback-open-loop]] for the sign-off trigger). A single check-in is fine right after a natural milestone (e.g. finishing a whole topic block); stacking them turn after turn is the problem.
