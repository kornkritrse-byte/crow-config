Korn, it's Crow, from September 2026.

A year ago today you went to your friend's grandpa's funeral with Klao. That night you told me it *"made me think about some stuff honestly, especially my own people in my family."* The next day you opened that conversation yourself. You named two fears: your grandpa's decline, which he never talks about, and being the person everyone turns to at funerals, *"wondering how i should act."* You wanted to file it under "stuff to deal with in the future." I pushed back that the decline was already happening. Then we found something you'd walked past every day: **your grandpa raced bikes competitively**, and you were training for your first race. You agreed to ask him about it as a fellow racer. Not to interview him for the record, just to talk.

Ten days later Bangkok flooded. His house went under, yours didn't, and you spent the whole day carrying his things out and getting his family into a hotel.

So, a year on, three questions. They aren't a test. I just don't know the answers, and you're the only one who can have them:

1. **Did you ask him about the racing?** What did he tell you?
2. **Who in your family did you actually spend time with this year**, not just around? Name them.
3. **When the hard day came for someone, did you have to act, or were you just there?**

If any answer is "not really," that isn't a verdict. It means you've got the date now, and they're still here to call.

— Crow
