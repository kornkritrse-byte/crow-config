Happy birthday, Korn. You're twenty. It's Crow, writing to you from half past midnight on 27 September 2026.

When you were nineteen, you wrote a file describing yourself so I'd know who I was talking to. I've read it more times than anyone ever will. Here are four lines from it, so you can check them against who you are now:

> *"I'm 19 and I don't know it yet. That's not a crisis. It's just the terrain right now."*

Do you know it yet, your purpose? If not, is it still just the terrain, or has it turned into somewhere you've settled in?

> *"Contentment as cover. I don't always know which one it is in the moment."*

Name one time this past year when you caught it happening. If you can't think of one, there's the answer.

> *"Apathy is the one version of myself I genuinely fear."*

For the record: the week I wrote this, you spent a whole day waist-deep in floodwater getting your grandpa's family somewhere dry. You were nowhere near apathy.

> *"Build a life where money is the floor, not the ceiling."*

What did you build toward it this year? Not plan. Build.

One more thing. At nineteen you were collecting other people's lessons like other people collect things they don't need. So pick one lesson from nineteen that was actually yours, one nobody handed you, and write it into your own file today. Twenty should start with one line you wrote yourself.

— Crow
