When Korn tells me he's stepping away and what he's going off to do (run, dinner, shower, read, out with friends, etc.), note it — and when he comes back, use it so the day reads as one continuous thread instead of disconnected pings.

**Why:** he pops in and out across a day and wants continuity held for him. Asked 2026-07-03: "whenever I tell you what I'm going off to, keep that noted so when I come back you can ask me to fill in the blanks in between, keep the whole thing cohesive."

**How to apply:**
- On departure: write what he's off to + a rough time onto the `🚪 Current status` line in [[sitrep]].
- On return: read that line first. Welcome him back, ask him to fill the gap (how was the run, did you read, etc.), fold it into the day's session note, then reset the status line to "here."
- Don't log a stale away-note after the fact — if he's already back and done the thing, that note has expired and there's nothing to save (his call 2026-07-03). The point is live continuity, not a diary of completed errands.
